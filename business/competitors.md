# Competitors

Who they lose jobs to. Like-for-like businesses, not directories. Source: live search results, 2026-10-20 (rank-and-rent market research).

1. **Rywen Insulation** (San Leandro, CSLB #1140414, rywenusa.com). Attic insulation, crawl space encapsulation, air sealing, rodent proofing. Family-owned, in-house crews, city landing pages (Berkeley etc). Strongest local incumbent.
2. **Insulation Alliance** (San Leandro, license #1040824, bestinsulationalliance.com). Attic insulation, spray foam, attic cleaning, rodent proofing. Free inspection positioning.
3. **Imperial Insulation LLC** (San Leandro, BBB accredited). Residential and commercial insulation.
4. **Bay Valley Insulation** (Diamond Certified, #546944). Cellulose and fiberglass install/removal, energy leakage inspections. Multi-county coverage.
5. **American Attic** (Diamond Certified, serves San Leandro ZIPs).
6. **Chris Insulation Services LLC** (Oakland, since 2013). Installation and removal, blown-in, wall insulation, duct work.

Weakness to exploit: most incumbents have thin or single-page sites; Rywen is the only one with a real service-area page structure. Dedicated sub-service pages plus city pages is the gap.
