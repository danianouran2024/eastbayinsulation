# Areas

- **Home city:** San Leandro, CA
- **Other towns they actually travel to:** Oakland, Hayward, Castro Valley, Alameda, Berkeley, Fremont, Union City, Newark, San Lorenzo, Albany, El Cerrito, Richmond, Piedmont, Emeryville, Livermore, Dublin, Pleasanton, Walnut Creek
- **Service radius:** about 30 miles around San Leandro
- **Source:** research report; all towns appear in incumbent contractors' published service lists

## oakland-ca
Oakland, CA. 8 miles north of the San Leandro base via I-880. Hilly East Oakland and the flats, Montclair, Rockridge and the downtown core.

## hayward-ca
Hayward, CA. 6 miles south via I-880. Flatland tracts, the hills above Cal State East Bay, and the industrial west side.

## castro-valley-ca
Castro Valley, CA. 6 miles east via I-580. Hillside homes on piers, many with crawl spaces.

## alameda-ca
Alameda, CA. 7 miles west. Island city with older Victorians and wartime tracts.

## berkeley-ca
Berkeley, CA. 15 miles north. Craftsman homes in the flats and hills.

## fremont-ca
Fremont, CA. 18 miles south. Large city spanning Mission San Jose to Warm Springs.

## union-city-ca
Union City, CA. 12 miles south via I-880.

## newark-ca
Newark, CA. 14 miles south.

## san-lorenzo-ca
San Lorenzo, CA. 3 miles south, directly adjacent to San Leandro.

## albany-ca
Albany, CA. 17 miles north.

## el-cerrito-ca
El Cerrito, CA. 18 miles north.

## richmond-ca
Richmond, CA. 20 miles northwest.

## piedmont-ca
Piedmont, CA. 10 miles north. Hillside homes surrounded by Oakland.

## emeryville-ca
Emeryville, CA. 12 miles northwest.

## livermore-ca
Livermore, CA. 25 miles east via I-580.

## dublin-ca
Dublin, CA. 20 miles southeast via I-580/I-680.

## pleasanton-ca
Pleasanton, CA. 22 miles southeast.

## walnut-creek-ca
Walnut Creek, CA. 25 miles northeast via CA-24/I-680.
