# Brand voice

- **Tone in three words:** plain, local, practical
- **Who reads the site and what worries them:** homeowners with damp crawl spaces, drafty attics, rodent damage or high energy bills; they worry about cost, mess and hiring the wrong crew.
- **Say / don't say:** say what is included and what drives the cost; never "cheapest", "cheap", "free removal", DIY coaching, or guaranteed savings numbers.
- **Sentence style:** short, declarative, no exclamation stacking, no hype words.
- **Phrases in their own words:** free inspection, written estimate, in-house crew.

## Never say (enforced by check-content --never)
cheapest, cheap, free removal, diy, handyman

## Always
- The next step in every hero: get a free estimate.
- Service pages talk about what is included, what drives the cost, local conditions (Bay Area moisture, marine layer), and what to expect.
- Area pages only state real facts: distance from San Leandro, which services we do there.
- No em dashes anywhere in copy.
